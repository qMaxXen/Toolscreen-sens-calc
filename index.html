DPI mode explanation

The DPI mode exists to help players who previously used the old Boateye method and have changed their mouse DPI.

With Toolscreen you can keep your original DPI and adjust only the toolscreen global sensitivity.

By entering your Old DPI (what you used with the old Boateye method) and your New DPI (what you want to use now or used before boateye), the calculator compensates for this difference and outputs a corrected Global Toolscreen sensitivity.

The New Minecraft sensitivity field remains fixed at 0.02291165 regardless of DPI, this is the most optimal value, as it has less pixel skip and higher 360 tolerance.

If you have not changed your DPI before setting up Toolscreen, you do not need to enable this mode.
